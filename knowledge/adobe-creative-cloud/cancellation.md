# How to cancel Adobe Creative Cloud

Service: Adobe Creative Cloud
Topic: cancellation
Source: https://helpx.adobe.com/account/individual/subscriptions-and-plans/renewals-and-cancellations/cancel-adobe-subscription.html
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

An Adobe subscription purchased directly from Adobe can be canceled from the Adobe account by opening Plans and payment, selecting Manage plan, and choosing Cancel your plan. Each active plan must be canceled separately.

Access to paid apps and services generally continues until the end of the current billing period. Afterward, the Adobe account remains active with free membership features, while paid application access and cloud-storage allowances are reduced.

Third-party purchases must be canceled through the provider that billed them. Cancellation fees and refund eligibility depend on the plan type and timing, so the cancellation summary should be reviewed before confirmation.
