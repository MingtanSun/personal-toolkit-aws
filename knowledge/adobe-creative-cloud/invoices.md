# Finding Adobe Creative Cloud invoices

Service: Adobe Creative Cloud
Topic: invoices
Source: https://helpx.adobe.com/account/individual/billing-and-payments/view-billing-and-invoices/view-download-email-adobe-invoice.html
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Individual Adobe customers can use their Adobe account to view, download, or email invoices for Creative Cloud, Acrobat, and other eligible purchases. Adobe states that up to three years of invoice history can be available in the account.

From Orders and invoices, the customer selects View invoices and then uses the available action to open, download, or email a PDF invoice. A specific order can be located by searching for its order number.

Teams customers normally access invoices through the Admin Console, and billing visibility can be limited to the primary administrator. Adobe does not provide automatic monthly invoice emails for every individual subscription.
