# Adobe Creative Cloud plan types

Service: Adobe Creative Cloud
Topic: plans
Source: https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/plans.html
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Adobe offers Creative Cloud plans for individuals, businesses, students and teachers, and educational institutions. Options can include access to a broad collection of Creative Cloud applications, a single application, photography products, Acrobat products, and additional services.

Payment commitments can include month-to-month, annual paid monthly, and annual prepaid arrangements. A lower displayed monthly payment does not necessarily mean the plan is month-to-month; it may be an annual commitment billed in installments.

Plan availability, included applications, generative-credit allowances, storage, prices, and promotional terms change over time. Users should compare the current localized plan page and review the commitment and cancellation terms before purchasing.
