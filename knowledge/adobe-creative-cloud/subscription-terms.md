# Adobe Creative Cloud subscription and refund terms

Service: Adobe Creative Cloud
Topic: refund-and-subscription-terms
Source: https://helpx.adobe.com/account/individual/terms-policies-and-regulations/adobe-subscription-terms.html
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Adobe distinguishes among annual plans paid monthly, annual prepaid plans, and month-to-month plans. These plans can have materially different early-cancellation consequences.

For many directly purchased plans, canceling within the initial refund period can qualify for a full refund. After that period, an annual plan paid monthly may carry an early termination fee based on the remaining commitment, while an annual prepaid plan may not provide a refund for the unused term. Month-to-month plans follow their own end-of-cycle terms.

The user's account and cancellation confirmation show the terms applying to the specific purchase. Country-specific terms and consumer laws can override or supplement the general United States policy.
