# Amazon Prime cancellation and refunds

Service: Amazon Prime
Topic: cancellation-and-refund
Source: https://www.amazon.com/amazonprime
Additional source: https://digprjsurvey.amazon.co.uk/csad/help/node/GTJQ7QZY7QL2HK4Y
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Amazon Prime members can end a membership from their Amazon account's Prime membership settings. The account presents the steps needed to confirm that the membership should not renew.

Refund eligibility depends on the marketplace, when the membership was purchased or renewed, and whether Prime benefits have been used during the current membership period. Amazon states that paid members who have not used eligible Prime benefits may qualify for a full refund of the current period; other cases may receive a different result under the applicable Prime terms.

Memberships obtained through a third party or a promotional or gift arrangement can follow different cancellation and refund rules. Users should review the terms for the Amazon marketplace where the membership was purchased.
