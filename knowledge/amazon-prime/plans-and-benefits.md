# Amazon Prime plans and benefits

Service: Amazon Prime
Topic: plans
Source: https://www.amazon.com/amazonprime
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Amazon Prime is a membership that can combine shopping, delivery, entertainment, and promotional benefits. Common benefits include eligible fast delivery, access to Prime Video content, selected music and podcast features, and member-only deals.

Amazon can offer monthly and annual membership options as well as special eligibility-based plans or trials. Available benefits, prices, and qualification requirements differ by country and can change.

Members should use their localized Amazon Prime page and account membership settings to confirm their exact plan, renewal price, included benefits, and next billing date.
