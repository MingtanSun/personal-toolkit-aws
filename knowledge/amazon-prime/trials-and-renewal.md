# Amazon Prime trials and automatic renewal

Service: Amazon Prime
Topic: trial-and-renewal
Source: https://www.amazon.com/amazonprime
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Eligible customers may be offered a Prime trial or promotional membership. Eligibility and trial length are determined by the offer shown to the customer.

Unless the offer states otherwise, a trial can convert into a paid membership and renew using the selected monthly or annual billing option. A member who does not want the paid membership must end the trial or disable renewal before the conversion date shown in the account.

The Prime membership page should be treated as the source of truth for the next charge date, renewal frequency, and current membership fee. Marketplace-specific terms control cancellation and refund eligibility after a trial converts.
