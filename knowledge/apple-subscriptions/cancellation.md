# How to cancel an Apple-billed subscription

Service: Apple Subscriptions
Topic: cancellation
Source: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

An Apple subscription or an eligible in-app subscription billed by the App Store can be canceled from the Subscriptions area of the Apple Account. On iPhone or iPad, the user opens Settings, selects their name, opens Subscriptions, selects the subscription, and chooses Cancel Subscription.

If no cancellation button appears and an expiration message is shown, the subscription may already be canceled. The Apple Account used for the purchase must be used to manage it.

When a receipt identifies another family member or Apple Account as the purchaser, that account owner generally needs to manage the subscription. Services billed outside Apple's system must be canceled with the service provider.
