# Sharing Apple subscriptions with a family

Service: Apple Subscriptions
Topic: family-sharing
Source: https://support.apple.com/en-us/105062
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Apple Family Sharing allows an organizer and up to five other family members to share eligible Apple services and App Store subscriptions while keeping separate Apple Accounts and personalized experiences.

Some eligible subscriptions are shared automatically, while services such as iCloud+ can require additional setup. Individual or student versions of certain services may not be shareable, and third-party developers decide whether their App Store subscriptions support sharing.

When purchase sharing is enabled, eligible family purchases use the organizer's shared payment arrangement. Family members can review which subscriptions are shared from Family settings.
