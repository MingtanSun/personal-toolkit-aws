# Viewing Apple purchase and subscription history

Service: Apple Subscriptions
Topic: billing-history
Source: https://support.apple.com/en-asia/guide/iphone/iph4e3e7324f/ios
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Apple purchase history includes paid and free apps, in-app purchases, subscriptions, media, books, AppleCare, and other eligible transactions associated with an Apple Account.

On iPhone, users can open the App Store account menu and select Apps and Purchase History, then Purchase History. Results can be narrowed by name, price, order identifier, date range, transaction type, and, for organizers, family member.

The Subscriptions page can be used to view active subscriptions, cancel, choose another available option, renew an expired subscription, or enable renewal receipt emails. The purchase history and subscription list should be checked when an unfamiliar charge appears.
