# Requesting an Apple purchase or subscription refund

Service: Apple Subscriptions
Topic: refund
Source: https://support.apple.com/en-gb/118223
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Some App Store, iTunes Store, Apple Books, subscription, and other Apple-service purchases may be eligible for a refund. A user can sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com, choose Request a refund, select a reason and the relevant purchase, and submit the request.

Apple reviews eligibility rather than guaranteeing every request. The request status is normally updated after review, and an approved refund can require additional time to reach the original payment method.

Pending charges cannot normally be submitted until a receipt is issued. Users should check which Apple Account appears on the receipt, because refund requests must be associated with the account that made the purchase.
