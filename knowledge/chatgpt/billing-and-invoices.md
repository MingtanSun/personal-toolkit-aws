# ChatGPT billing dates and invoices

Service: ChatGPT
Topic: billing-and-invoices
Source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9039756-managing-billing-settings-on-chatgpt-web-and-platform
Additional source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8156167
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

ChatGPT subscriptions and OpenAI API usage use separate billing systems. For an eligible web subscription, users manage the payment method and available billing history from ChatGPT Settings and Billing. Workspace billing is available to authorized owners or administrators.

A monthly ChatGPT subscription normally renews on the calendar date when the user originally subscribed. OpenAI does not support changing that invoice date directly.

Invoices for subscriptions purchased through an app store are provided by Apple or Google rather than the ChatGPT web billing portal. Users should confirm which platform issued the charge before searching for an invoice.
