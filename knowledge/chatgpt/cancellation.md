# How to cancel a ChatGPT subscription

Service: ChatGPT
Topic: cancellation
Source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7232927-how-do-i-cancel
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

The cancellation method depends on where the subscription was purchased. A personal subscription bought on chatgpt.com is managed from ChatGPT Settings and Billing. Apple App Store and Google Play subscriptions must be canceled through the corresponding store.

Canceling stops future renewal but does not automatically refund previous charges. Paid features normally remain available until the end of the current billing period. OpenAI recommends canceling at least 24 hours before the next billing date.

Uninstalling the ChatGPT app does not cancel a subscription. Self-service ChatGPT Business subscriptions are managed by an authorized workspace owner from Workspace settings and Billing.
