# ChatGPT subscription plans

Service: ChatGPT
Topic: plans
Source: https://openai.com/chatgpt/pricing
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

ChatGPT offers individual and organizational plans. Depending on the market and current product lineup, options can include Free, paid individual tiers such as Go, Plus, or Pro, and workspace plans such as Business, Enterprise, and Edu.

Plans differ in model access, usage limits, tools, file handling, voice capabilities, research features, administrative controls, privacy settings, and support. Organizational plans can add workspace management, security, and compliance features.

Plan names, prices, model availability, feature limits, and promotions change frequently. The current localized pricing page and the user's Billing page should be used as the source of truth before subscribing or changing plans.
