# Requesting a ChatGPT subscription refund

Service: ChatGPT
Topic: refund
Source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7232895-how-do-i-request-a-refund-for-chatgpt-plus
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Refund requests for eligible subscriptions purchased on chatgpt.com or through Google Play can be started through OpenAI Support while signed in to the ChatGPT account associated with the charge.

Apple manages purchases made through Apple subscriptions, so those cancellation and refund requests must be submitted to Apple. Eligibility depends on the purchase, timing, location, and applicable terms or consumer law.

OpenAI describes additional regional rights for certain customers, including residents of the European Union, United Kingdom, Turkey, and Korea. Users should review the current official article because eligibility periods and processing rules can change.
