# The referrals policy in Codecademy

Service: Codecademy
Topic: referrals
Source: https://help.codecademy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360022155773-Codecademy-Referrals
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

For every friend you refer who purchases a Codecademy membership, you'll receive a gift card equal to $20 USD. Your reward will be sent around 14 days after your friend's purchase.

You can access our referral page by scrolling to the bottom of Codecademy.com and clicking "Refer a friend". You can sign up for our referral program and receive a unique referral link to share with your friends and family.

Your friend will be granted a discount code for 50% off an annual Codecademy subscription. Follow the link provided, and the code will automatically apply at checkout.
