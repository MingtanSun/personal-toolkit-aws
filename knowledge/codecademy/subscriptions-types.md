## Free trial
Codecademy Pro and Plus subscriptions include members-only content, mobile app, quizzes, community support, and real-world projects. New Codecademy accounts are eligible to receive a free trial of Codecademy Pro or Plus. A valid credit or debit card is required to receive a trial, and accounts are charged after day 7 of the trial.

To maintain access to these features after 7 days, you need a paid subscription. If you choose not to upgrade, progress made during trial will not be lost however your quizzes, paths, and projects will be unavailable until you upgrade. If you want to hold onto your projects, we recommend pushing them to Github or saving them on your personal computer.

## Plus, Pro and All Access Path
The Codecademy Pro, Plus, and All Access Pass subscriptions give you access to every course in our catalog. A Plus subscription will also give you access to many Skill Paths while our Pro subscriptions will give you access to all Skill Paths and Career Paths.  For more on the difference between Pro and Plus, please see here.

An All Access Pass gives learners access to eligible Bootcamps, Workshops, and Codecademy Pro.

There is no limit to the amount of courses or paths you can take at a given time. With Pro, Plus, and All Access Pass you will also get access to exclusive features and tools that will help accelerate and develop your learning:

Personalized Learning Paths

We offer Paths (Career and Skill Paths) to follow based on your goals and interests – e.g., Web Development (Front-end, Back-end, and Full-Stack), Data Science, and Computer Science.

Access to Quizzes

Quizzes are a great way to test how much you’ve learned and how much you’re retaining. With Codecademy Pro, you can quiz yourself at regular intervals to make sure you’re ready to move on to the next topic.

Real World Projects

With programming, there’s no substitute for actually building something. We’ve created realistic projects to teach you how to apply what you’re learning in a hands-on, practical way. Completing projects will help you solidify your learning.

Access to Practice

You will have access to a practice tab, where you can access a number of projects and quizzes to practice your skills on a daily basis.

Mobile App

Codecademy Go is a mobile app and supplemental tool exclusive to your Codecademy Pro or Plus subscription. With Codecademy Go you can practice coding, review coding concepts, and read articles and stories that will get you closer to achieving your learning goals.

Videos

We’ve added project walkthrough videos in the learning environment for over 100 on-platform Pro projects. A coding expert will work through projects step by step to drill the concepts. You can review these again and again!

AI Builder

Each subscription type will grant you a certain number of credits to use the AI Builder feature on Codecademy and reset every month! More information on the AI Builder HERE.
