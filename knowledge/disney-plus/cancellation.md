# How to cancel Disney+

Service: Disney+
Topic: cancellation
Source: https://help.disneyplus.com/en-GB/article/disneyplus-en-pt-cancel
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Subscribers billed directly by Disney+ can cancel through a web or mobile browser by opening Profile, Account, selecting the Disney+ subscription, and choosing Cancel Subscription.

Cancellation generally takes effect at the end of the current billing period or any agreed minimum term. Access and billing can continue until that point, after which no further direct renewal charge should be made.

Canceling the subscription does not automatically delete the Disney or MyDisney account. Subscribers billed by Apple, Google, Amazon, or another partner must follow that provider's cancellation procedure.
