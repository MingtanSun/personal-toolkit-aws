# Disney+ charges and refunds

Service: Disney+
Topic: billing-and-refund
Source: https://help.disneyplus.com/en-GB/article/disneyplus-en-vc-view-charges
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Directly billed Disney+ subscribers can review recent charges in Billing History on the Account page. Selecting a charge shows details such as the amount, applicable tax, and billing date. An active subscription may be required to access this page.

Disney+ may issue refunds in approved circumstances. For a refund approved by Disney+ Support, the amount is generally returned to the original payment method, and the billing history can show a summary of the refund.

Subscribers billed through a third party must follow that provider's refund policy and processing timeline. The presence of a cancellation does not by itself guarantee a refund.
