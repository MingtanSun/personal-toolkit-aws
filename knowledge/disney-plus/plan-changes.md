# Changing a Disney+ plan

Service: Disney+
Topic: plan-management
Source: https://help.disneyplus.com/en-GB/article/disneyplus-en-bz-manage-subscription
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Directly billed subscribers can change an eligible Disney+ plan from the Account page under Plans and Billing. The user selects the current subscription, chooses Change, reviews the available options, and confirms the new plan.

An upgrade to a higher-tier plan may take effect immediately. Changing between monthly and annual billing for the same plan can take effect on the next billing date. A plan change may end an existing promotion or price hold.

Third-party billing partners can restrict which plans are offered and control how changes are made. In those cases, the subscriber should manage the plan through that partner.
