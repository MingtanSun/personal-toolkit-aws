# Hulu annual billing policy

Service: Hulu
Topic: annual-billing
Source: https://help.hulu.com/article/hulu-annual-billing
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Annual billing is available for eligible Hulu plans and directly billed subscribers. Hulu currently limits annual billing to specified plan types, while Live TV, premium, bundle, and add-on products may continue to use monthly billing.

Changing from an eligible monthly plan to annual billing can create an immediate charge, while some downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. An annual plan renews automatically one year after its billing date unless canceled beforehand.

Canceling an annual subscription normally preserves access until the annual term ends. Hulu states that refunds are not issued merely because an annual subscription is canceled. Third-party-billed subscribers may not be eligible for Hulu's direct annual option.
