# How to cancel Hulu

Service: Hulu
Topic: cancellation
Source: https://help.hulu.com/article/hulu-cancel-hulu-subscription
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

A subscriber billed directly by Hulu can cancel from the Account page by selecting Cancel under Your Subscription and completing the confirmation flow. Hulu may offer a temporary pause before the final cancellation step.

For most subscriptions, access continues until the end of the current billing cycle and future charges stop. Some trial and Live TV arrangements can end access sooner, so the confirmation screen should be reviewed carefully.

Cancellation does not delete the user's Hulu or related Disney account. Subscribers billed through Apple, Amazon, a mobile provider, or another third party must follow that provider's cancellation process.
