# Hulu past and upcoming charges

Service: Hulu
Topic: billing
Source: https://help.hulu.com/contact-us/account-and-billing-unexpected-charges
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Hulu subscriptions may be billed monthly or annually, depending on the plan. Taxes can apply, and a plan change or newly selected add-on can result in a prorated charge.

Directly billed subscribers can review scheduled charges from the Upcoming Charges area of the Hulu Account page and use View History to inspect prior payments.

Unexpected charges can also result from bundles, add-ons, plan changes, or a separate account. Subscribers should first confirm the billing provider and active products shown on the Account page before contacting support.
