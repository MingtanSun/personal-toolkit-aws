# Hulu plans, bundles, and add-ons

Service: Hulu
Topic: plans-and-addons
Source: https://help.hulu.com/category/plans-and-add-ons
Additional source: https://help.hulu.com/article/hulu-annual-billing
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Hulu offers multiple subscription arrangements, including on-demand plans, eligible annual billing, Live TV products, bundles, and optional premium or partner add-ons. Advertising, live channels, downloads, simultaneous streams, and included services vary by plan.

Directly billed subscribers can manage eligible plans and add-ons from the Your Subscription section of the Account page. A third-party billing provider may limit available changes and require plan management through its own service.

Plan names, prices, bundle combinations, and promotions change over time. The Hulu Account page and current plans page should be used to confirm the exact offering before a purchase or change.
