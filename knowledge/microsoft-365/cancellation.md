# How to cancel a Microsoft 365 subscription

Service: Microsoft 365
Topic: cancellation
Source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-US/accounts-billing/subscriptions/cancel-your-microsoft-subscription
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

A subscription purchased directly from Microsoft can be managed from the Microsoft account Services and subscriptions page. The purchaser signs in with the account used for the order, selects Manage for the subscription, and follows the Cancel or Upgrade or Cancel flow.

If the page shows Turn on recurring billing, renewal is already disabled and the subscription is scheduled to expire on the displayed date. The subscriber can normally continue using paid benefits until expiration.

Subscriptions purchased through Apple, Google Play, Amazon, or another retailer must be canceled through that billing provider. Cancellation and prorated-refund rights vary by country and applicable consumer law.
