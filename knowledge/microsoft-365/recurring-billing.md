# Microsoft 365 recurring billing

Service: Microsoft 365
Topic: recurring-billing
Source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-US/accounts-billing/subscriptions/turn-recurring-billing-on-or-off-for-a-microsoft-subscription
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Turning off recurring billing stops future automatic renewal but normally leaves the subscription and its benefits active until the displayed expiration date. When recurring billing is enabled, Microsoft charges on the renewal date using the plan's monthly or annual cycle.

Members can manage recurring billing from Services and subscriptions by selecting Manage for the relevant subscription. Seeing Turn on recurring billing means automatic renewal is already off.

Eligible directly billed subscriptions may allow switching between monthly and annual payment frequency. Third-party providers control recurring billing for subscriptions purchased through their stores.
