# Microsoft 365 subscription refunds

Service: Microsoft 365
Topic: refund
Source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accounts-billing/subscriptions/how-to-get-a-refund-on-a-microsoft-subscription
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

To check refund eligibility for an eligible Microsoft 365 subscription purchased directly from Microsoft, the customer normally begins by canceling it from the Services and subscriptions page. Not every cancellation produces a refund.

Eligibility depends on factors such as the subscription, purchase timing, country, and local law. Microsoft identifies certain countries where some cancellations may qualify for a prorated refund.

Apple App Store, Google Play, and other third-party purchases are controlled by those providers, so Microsoft generally cannot issue their refunds. Users should sign in with the same Microsoft account used for the original purchase.
