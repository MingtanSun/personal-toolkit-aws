# Managing a Microsoft 365 subscription

Service: Microsoft 365
Topic: subscription-management
Source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accounts-billing/subscriptions/where-can-i-manage-my-microsoft-365-subscription
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

The correct management location depends on who billed the subscription. A Microsoft-billed subscription is managed from the Microsoft account Services and subscriptions page, where the owner can review renewal information, change payment details, switch eligible plans, manage sharing, or cancel.

Subscriptions purchased through Apple, Google Play, Amazon, or another retailer are managed by that provider. Microsoft may be unable to cancel, refund, or modify those subscriptions.

The user should first sign in with the Microsoft account used for the purchase and inspect the available management controls. Missing controls can indicate the wrong account or a third-party billing arrangement.
