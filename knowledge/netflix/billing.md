# Netflix billing and payment information

Service: Netflix
Topic: billing
Source: https://help.netflix.com/en/node/41049
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Netflix normally charges a membership once per month at the beginning of the billing cycle. The billing date generally corresponds to the date the member originally signed up. When that calendar date does not occur in a month, Netflix charges on the last day of that month.

Members can view their plan, subscription price, taxes, and past charges from Payment History on the Account page. Invoices can also be printed from the billing area. Charges can take several days to appear, and time-zone differences may shift the displayed date by one day.

Accounts billed through a third party can have a different billing date and must often manage payment details through that provider.
