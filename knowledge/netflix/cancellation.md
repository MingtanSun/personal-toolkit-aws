# How to cancel Netflix

Service: Netflix
Topic: cancellation
Source: https://help.netflix.com/en/node/407
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Netflix members can cancel at any time from the Manage Membership page by choosing Cancel and then Finish Cancellation. Signing out or deleting the Netflix app does not cancel a membership.

For most active accounts, cancellation stops future renewal charges and access continues until the end of the current billing period. If the account is on hold, cancellation can take effect immediately. Members paying through a partner must follow that provider's cancellation process.

Gift-card or promotional balances may keep the account active until the remaining balance is used. Netflix also offers a temporary membership pause for some eligible plans and payment methods.
