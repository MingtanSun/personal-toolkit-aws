# Netflix gift cards and balances

Service: Netflix
Topic: gift-cards
Source: https://help.netflix.com/en/node/32950
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Netflix gift cards can be redeemed toward a streaming subscription. They do not expire, cannot be reloaded, and are generally non-refundable. Multiple cards can be redeemed to increase the available account balance.

Netflix uses the gift-card balance for membership charges before charging a saved payment method. If no other payment method is present, access continues until the balance is exhausted. A plan change can alter how quickly the balance is consumed.

Gift cards normally must use the same currency as the Netflix account. Some partner-billed accounts cannot use a Netflix gift card until the partner billing arrangement ends.
