# Managing a Netflix plan

Service: Netflix
Topic: plan-management
Source: https://help.netflix.com/en/node/102377
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Netflix members can review and change their membership plan from the Account page. Plan choice can affect video quality, simultaneous streams, downloads, advertising, and eligibility for extra-member slots.

An upgrade can affect billing immediately, while the timing of a downgrade can depend on the account and billing arrangement. Members whose Netflix access is included in a partner package may need to manage plan changes through that provider.

Because plan names, prices, and availability are country-specific and can change, members should use the plan comparison shown in their own Netflix account for current details.
