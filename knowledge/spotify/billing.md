# Spotify billing dates and receipts

Service: Spotify
Topic: billing
Source: https://support.spotify.com/us/article/billing-date/
Additional source: https://support.spotify.com/us/article/check-receipts/
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Spotify's billing cycle begins on the date a member subscribes. The next billing date is shown on the account page and can also be viewed in the app under Settings and privacy, Account, and Billing.

Spotify does not provide a direct way to change the billing date. A member who needs a different date must cancel, wait until the account changes to Spotify Free, and resubscribe on the preferred date. Duo and Family members may need to be invited again afterward.

Plan and audiobook receipts are available from Payment History, generally for transactions from the previous two years. A receipt can be viewed and downloaded from the payment details.
