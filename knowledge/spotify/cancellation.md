# How to cancel Spotify Premium

Service: Spotify
Topic: cancellation
Source: https://support.spotify.com/us/article/cancel-premium/
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

A directly billed Spotify Premium plan can be canceled from the account page under Manage your plan. Premium access normally remains active until the next billing date, after which the account changes to Spotify Free. Playlists and saved music remain available on the free account.

Canceling during a zero-price free trial can return the account to Spotify Free immediately. For Duo and Family plans, only the plan manager can cancel the whole plan; an ordinary member can leave but cannot cancel it for everyone.

If Spotify shows that the plan is managed by a mobile carrier, internet provider, or another partner, cancellation must be completed through that partner.
