# Spotify Premium plan types

Service: Spotify
Topic: plans
Source: https://www.spotify.com/us/premium/
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Spotify commonly offers Premium Individual, Student, Duo, and Family plans. Individual and Student are designed for one eligible account. Duo is intended for two people residing at the same address, while Family supports multiple eligible household members under a plan manager.

Premium plans generally include ad-free music, offline listening, greater playback control, and other features advertised for the member's market. Audiobook allowances, student benefits, trial offers, and eligibility rules can differ by plan and country.

Current prices and promotions change over time, so users should consult Spotify's localized Premium page or their account page before making a purchase or plan change.
