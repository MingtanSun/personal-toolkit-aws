# Spotify Premium refund policy

Service: Spotify
Topic: refund
Source: https://support.spotify.com/us/article/refund-policy/
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Canceling Spotify Premium normally stops future renewals but does not refund the already-paid billing period. The account generally keeps Premium benefits until that period ends and then moves to Spotify Free.

Spotify cannot refund store-bought gift cards. Payments made through a partner are controlled by that partner, so refund requests must be directed to the billing provider shown on the Spotify account page.

Separate rules can apply to audiobook purchases, listening-hour top-ups, podcast subscriptions, fraudulent transactions, and rights provided by local consumer law.
