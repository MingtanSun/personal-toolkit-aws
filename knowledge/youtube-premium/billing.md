# YouTube Premium billing details and receipts

Service: YouTube Premium
Topic: billing
Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10217125?hl=en
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Users can review their membership type, current status, price, payment method, and next billing date from YouTube's Purchases and memberships area or the Paid Memberships page.

Monthly members see the next billing-cycle date, while trial members see the trial end date. Apple-billed subscriptions are identified as billed by Apple and must be managed through Apple.

Receipts are available in Google Payments under Activity. Transaction details include the amount, date and time, payment status, and payment method. Tax invoices may be available in supported regions.
