# How to cancel YouTube Premium

Service: YouTube Premium
Topic: cancellation
Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6308278?hl=en
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

A YouTube Premium or YouTube Music Premium membership can be canceled from the Paid Memberships page by opening Manage membership, selecting Deactivate, and continuing through the cancellation steps.

Cancellation turns off automatic renewal. Benefits normally remain available until the end of the current billing period, and the account is not charged again unless the user subscribes again.

Memberships billed by Apple, Google Play, or another partner must be managed through the relevant billing provider. Users can check their paid-membership page to confirm whether the subscription is already canceled.
