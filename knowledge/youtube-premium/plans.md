# YouTube Premium membership types

Service: YouTube Premium
Topic: plans
Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16475192?hl=en
Additional source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11417260?hl=en
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

YouTube offers several paid membership arrangements, including recurring individual plans, eligible multi-person plans, student memberships, and prepaid plans in supported markets. YouTube Premium, Premium Lite, and YouTube Music Premium provide different sets of benefits.

Recurring individual plans renew monthly. Family and other multi-person plans have household and group requirements. Student plans require eligibility verification and may require periodic re-verification.

Annual plans are prepaid, non-recurring, and available only to eligible individual users in supported countries. They expire after the purchased term, cannot be paused, and are generally not refundable.
