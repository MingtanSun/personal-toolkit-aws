# YouTube Premium refund policy

Service: YouTube Premium
Topic: refund
Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12014038?hl=en
Retrieved at: 2026-09-11

Canceling a recurring YouTube Premium or YouTube Music Premium membership normally stops future renewal but does not refund the unused portion between cancellation and the end of the billing cycle.

A user may be eligible to request a refund when purchased videos or Premium features do not work as expected. If a refund is approved, Premium access ends and the payment is returned according to the applicable processing timeline.

Google Play-billed purchases follow the Google Play process. Apple-billed purchases are subject to Apple's refund rules. Partially used prepaid plans are generally not refundable, and annual plans have separate restrictions.
